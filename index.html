Our purpose

The Sustainable Energy Club builds an inclusive community of students who learn, debate, and act to power our world without warming it.

You don't need to know anything about energy to join, just show up curious. We keep meetings hands-on and actually do things instead of just talking about them.

What we do

The three big things we do.

  1. 01

    Learn and debate energy sources

    We dig into the good and bad of different clean energy sources and do hands-on activities so it doesn't feel like another class.

    • Solar
    • Wind
    • Hydro
    • Nuclear
  2. 02

    Understand the grid and the data

    We figure out how the electricity grid actually works and look at real energy data to see when and why people use power.

    Load shapes
    How much electricity people use, graphed over different time periods.
    Baseload vs. peak load
    Things that run 24/7 (like a fridge) vs. short bursts (like an AC).
    Time-of-use pricing
    Why the same electricity costs more or less depending on the time of day, week, or year.
  3. 03

    The Community Powering Partners Project

    Our biggest project. We team up with an LWHS Center and help them figure out where they're wasting energy, and how to fix it.

    See how it works

Flagship project

Community Powering Partners.

This is where everything we learn actually gets used. We measure, dig through the data, and build them something they can keep using after we're done.

  1. 1

    Team up

    Find 1 or 2 LWHS Centers who want to work with us.

  2. 2

    Go investigate

    Look at their PG&E data and visit in person to figure out what's eating up energy, then set up smart plugs to track it.

  3. 3

    Build a dashboard

    Turn all that data into a dashboard we can actually read.

  4. 4

    Give recommendations

    Show them what to change to save energy (and money).

  5. 5

    Leave a tool behind

    Set them up with a website to keep uploading data and see their progress over time.
