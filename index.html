Our purpose

The Sustainable Energy Club builds an inclusive community of students who learn, debate, and act to power our world without warming it.

No prior expertise required — just curiosity. We keep things hands-on, argue in good faith, and turn what we learn into action.

What we do

Three ways we dig in.

  1. 01

    Learn & debate energy sources

    We weigh the real pros and cons of clean and low-emission energy — with hands-on activities so it never turns into a lecture.

    • Solar
    • Wind
    • Hydro
    • Nuclear
  2. 02

    Read the grid like a language

    We learn how the electricity grid works and analyze real usage data to see how demand actually behaves.

    Load shapes
    Electricity demand plotted across different time frames.
    Baseload vs. peak load
    Steady 24/7 draw (a refrigerator) vs. short spikes (an air conditioner).
    Time-of-use pricing
    Why the same kilowatt-hour costs more or less by hour, day, and season.
  3. 03

    The Community Powering Partners Project

    Our flagship, get-your-hands-dirty project: partner with an LWHS Center and help them find — and fix — where energy is being wasted.

Flagship project

Community Powering Partners.

We take everything we've learned and put it to work for real partners — measuring, analyzing, and leaving behind something that keeps helping after we're gone.

  1. 1

    Partner up

    Recruit 1–2 LWHS Center partners to work with the club.

  2. 2

    Investigate on site

    Take their PG&E data and visit in person to spot culprits and set up smart-plug analysis.

  3. 3

    Build the dashboard

    Create and analyze a data dashboard from what we measure.

  4. 4

    Present recommendations

    Turn the analysis into clear, doable changes for the partner.

  5. 5

    Leave a living tool

    Build an ongoing website partners use to upload data and track impact over time.
