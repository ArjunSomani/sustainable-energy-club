Purpose

Why we exist

The LWHS Sustainable Energy Club builds an inclusive community of students who learn, debate, and act to power our world without warming it.

Goals

What we do

01

Learn & debate energy sources

Dig into the pros and cons of various clean and low-emission energy sources — with hands-on activities to keep it engaging.

  • ☀️ Solar
  • 💨 Wind
  • 💧 Hydro
  • ⚛️ Nuclear
02

Understand the grid & the data

Learn how the energy grid works and analyze real energy-usage data to see how demand actually behaves.

  • Load shapes Demand for electricity across different time frames.
  • Baseload vs. peak load Continuous 24/7 demand (refrigerators) vs. short bursts (air conditioners).
  • Time-of-use pricing How the cost of electricity varies by time of day, week, and year.
03

LWHS Community Powering Partners Project

Our flagship, hands-on project: team up with LWHS Center partners and help them cut energy waste with real measurement and analysis.

  1. Partner upRecruit 1–2 LWHS Center partners to work with the club.
  2. InvestigateTake their PG&E data and visit onsite to identify culprits and set up smart-plug analysis.
  3. AnalyzeCreate and analyze a data dashboard.
  4. RecommendPresent clear, actionable recommendations.
  5. SustainBuild an ongoing website for them to upload data and track impact over time.

Flagship project

From classroom to real impact

The Community Powering Partners Project turns everything we learn into action. Students work directly with community partners — measuring, analyzing, and recommending — then leave behind a living dashboard so partners can track their impact long after our visit.
